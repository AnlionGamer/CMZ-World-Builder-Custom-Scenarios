# Changelog — Undead Fortresses

## v1.3.2

- Finalized terrain-integrated fortress foundations.
- Replaced the universal non-Hell Bloodstone structural floor with Rock.
- Added shallow solid footing and deep-gap Rock deck/support-pier behavior.
- Finalized coverage-balanced fortress placement to remove fixed-bearing star/spoke distribution.
- Preserved the fixed 5,200m authored range through the end of the first full Hell ring.
- Preserved Sparse, Standard, Dense, and Very Dense density options.
- Preserved Fortress Near Spawn, 30 archetypes, 12 exterior profiles, native encounters, native loot, mirroring, and vanilla-compatible world deltas.
- Promoted the runtime-approved foundation and placement behavior to the official v1.3.2 release.

## v1.3.0

- Established the fixed 5,200m authored range.
- Added World Builder `official-cmz` foundation integration.
- Retained Very Dense and Fortress Near Spawn options.
